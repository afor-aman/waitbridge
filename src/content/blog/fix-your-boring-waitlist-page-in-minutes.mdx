## What a Waitlist Page That Converts Actually Needs

After reviewing dozens of high-performing pages, the pattern is clear:

### 1. One sharp headline  
Say what you do and who it’s for. Skip "Join our waitlist."

### 2. A strong visual background  
Gradient, image, or patterns. This alone makes your page feel real.

### 3. One-field form  
Email only. Anything else kills conversions.

### 4. One clear CTA button  
High contrast. "Get Early Access" beats "Submit" every time.

### 5. A log is a must
So people can recognize and relate to your brand

This is basic **startup waitlist best practices**, yet most founders ignore them.
